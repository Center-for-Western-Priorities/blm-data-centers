# Corporate Ownership Research — BLM Data Center ROW Applicants
Research conducted 2026-09-09 into entities behind BLM ROW applications in AZ, ID, OR, UT, WY.

**Note on documents/:** No PDFs were downloaded for this workstream. Every accessible state portal (Idaho, Wyoming, Oregon, Delaware) displays filing data on-screen but gates certified copies/document images behind a paid order ($10–20/doc). The `documents/<entity-slug>/` folders created for this task remain empty.

## Top-line answers

**1. Copia Digital LLC's home state / registered agent, and Leo Data LLC comparison**
Confirmed: Copia Digital, LLC is a **Delaware** LLC, independently confirmed via Idaho, Wyoming, and Oregon foreign-qualification filings, all sharing principal address **24853 Del Prado, Dana Point, CA 92629** — the HQ of **Copia Power**, a Carlyle-backed power/AI-infrastructure developer being sold to EQT for $2.6B (announced July 2026). Oregon's record names the **manager as "Copia Development, LLC"** at the same address — direct documentary proof of the corporate family link. Registered agents: C T Corporation System (WY, OR); no agent of record in Idaho (revoked, "Agent Resigned or Invalid").
Leo Data LLC comparison: **could not be tested** — Arizona's ACC portal is CAPTCHA-gated (not bypassed, per rules) and no registered-agent name for Leo Data LLC could be found anywhere. Confidence: **no evidence either way**.

**2. Who's behind Prometheus Hyperscale, and its main site**
Confirmed, high confidence: Founded 2020 by **Trenton Thornock** as "Wyoming Hyperscale White Box," rebranded 2024 to Prometheus Hyperscale. **Bernard Looney** (former BP CEO) became Chairman/CEO April 1, 2026; Thornock moved to advisor. Also: Trevor Neilson (President), Marc Chennault (CFO), both also officers of record in Wyoming's corporate filing. Main site: the flagship **Evanston, Wyoming** campus (1.25GW+), which almost certainly corresponds to the Kemmerer FO infrastructure cases (Altamont Road access road/telecom/water, filed 2025-02-18) — Prometheus's WY corporate filing lists its principal office at 8936 County Road 180, Evanston, WY. This ID is strong circumstantial, not case-name-confirmed. A second, separate Prometheus project near Casper, WY (Natrona/Converse county) is unrelated to the Kemmerer cases.

**3. Is Wyoming Hyperscale White Box LLC's facility already operational?**
Confirmed via corporate lineage, high confidence: "White Box" is the same venture's original name, not a separate still-operating company — it became Prometheus Hyperscale in 2024. The two small BLM infrastructure ROWs (0.7-ac water facility, 9.5-ac access road) closing 2024-07-09 aligns with an already-built ranch-scale prototype, right as the company rebranded and scaled up. Wyoming's own registry shows no LLC named "Wyoming Hyperscale White Box" — only the surviving corp (2020-000932792, now "Prometheus Hyperscale") with a same-day "Conversion" filing (10-28-2024) coinciding with dissolution of a duplicate placeholder filing — suggestive, not documentary-proof, of that conversion.
