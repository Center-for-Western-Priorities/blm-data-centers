DOCKET E-00000A-25-0069
===========================================================================
Company Name (nominal): Arizona Corporation Commission
Docket Type: Electric / Generic
Status: Open
Filed: 04/02/2025
Description: "In the Matter of the Commission's Inquiry and Review of the Existing Rate Classifications and
other Potential Issues relating to Data Centers."

This is a GENERIC POLICY docket (workshops, comment letters), not a project-specific docket. It contains no
applicant-level detail (no project names, MW figures tied to specific sites, or counties for individual
projects). Reviewed the Documents tab (3 pages); entries seen were consumer/interest-group comment letters
(RUCO, APS, SRP, TEP, Goldwater Institute, Interwest Energy Alliance, Capital Power, Arizona PIRG, various
academic/NGO commenters on data center water/energy impacts, some "Rejected - Miscellaneous Comments" entries).
NONE of Frontier / Harquahala / TAP / Copia Digital / Leo Data / Copia appear in the document descriptions,
filer names, or "filed for" names on the pages reviewed.

A related news summary (via WebFetch of azcc.gov news page, see below) states aggregate MW figures from an
April 16, 2026 workshop in this docket:
  - SRP: 59 large-load customers, "nearly 7,000 MW combined load" (no project-level breakdown given)
  - APS: peak demand for large customers "approximately 13.1 gigawatts this year" (aggregate figure only)
  - TEP/UNS: one data center project under contract, "estimated load forecast of 286 MW" (unnamed);
    other prospective TEP/UNS data center projects forecast to require "about 800 MW" (unnamed)
  - Statewide: ~2,000 MW operating data center capacity, "more than 10,600 MW of additional capacity planned"
  - Quote: "APS and SRP have developed their own queue process for whittling down prospective data center
    customers" -- i.e., this queue is NOT the same as the public FERC generator-interconnection queue, and
    is not published with applicant names.
Source URL: https://www.azcc.gov/news/home/2026/04/20/acc-large-load-data-center-workshop-highlights

DOCKET E-01773A-26-0123
===========================================================================
Company Name: Arizona Electric Power Cooperative, Inc. (AEPCO)
Docket Type: Electric / Joint Application
Status: Compliance Due
Filed: 04/16/2026
Description: "In the matter of the joint application for a streamlined process for multiparty agreements for
electric service to large-load customers submitted by Arizona Electric Power Cooperative, Inc., Duncan Valley
Electric Cooperative, Inc., Graham County Electric Cooperative, Inc., Mohave Electric Cooperative, Inc.,
Sulphur Springs Valley Electric Cooperative, Inc., and Trico Electric Cooperative, Inc."
"There is 6 companies involved with this case."

Decision: ACC approved this application unanimously at its 08/12/2026 Open Meeting (Decision #82128 posted
08/27/2026 per the Documents tab).

This is ALSO a generic PROCESS/FRAMEWORK docket -- it creates a standardized approval pathway the co-ops
(including Mohave Electric Cooperative, which serves the Lake Havasu City / Bullhead City / Kingman corridor)
will use for FUTURE large-load service agreements. It does not itself identify any specific large-load
customer, project, county, or MW figure. Individual large-load service agreements negotiated under this new
process would presumably be filed as SEPARATE dockets (one per customer/utility) once/if they occur; no such
follow-on docket naming Frontier / Harquahala / TAP / Copia Digital / Leo Data was found in the time available.
News coverage (mohavedailynews.com, azfreenews.com, ktar.com, kjzz.org, gilavalleycentral.net,
bigcountrynewsconnection.com) confirms: "The utilities said they are already in talks with several large-load
businesses looking to build facilities in their service areas" -- but no business names were reported.

===========================================================================
CONCLUSION FOR THIS PROJECT
===========================================================================
Neither ACC docket reviewed contains project-, applicant-, or site-specific information matching any of the
four BLM filings (Frontier, Harquahala, TAP, Leo Data / Copia Digital). Both are policy/process dockets. If a
specific Mohave Electric Cooperative or APS large-load service agreement docket exists for one of these named
projects, it was not surfaced by keyword/company search in the time available -- ACC eDocket's search UI is an
autocomplete-driven company/DBA picker rather than a full-text search, which limits the ability to search by an
applicant name unless that name is already a party of record.
